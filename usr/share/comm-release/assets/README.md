`owl.ansi` is the new BigCommunity owl, pre-rendered as 24 × 10 braille cells.
Source: `/usr/share/icons/manjaro/bigcomm-text-dark-rounded.svg` from
comm-gnome-config, the same logo used by the current bigcommunity-fastfetch.
Generated with that project's `tools/make-logo.py --rows 10`.
The SVG's colors are preserved; no image renderer is needed at runtime.
